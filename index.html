What is pantbrev?

Pantbrev is a mortgage deed used as collateral for the bank loan. When existing pantbrev in the property is not enough for your new mortgage, you need new pantbrev. New pantbrev has a state stamp duty of 2% of the new pantbrev amount plus a registration fee (commonly 375 SEK per application).

You can enter existing pantbrev to reduce the amount of new pantbrev needed. The calculator only charges pantbrev on the remaining part of the loan that is not covered by existing pantbrev.

Other common Swedish costs included

Lagfart (title registration) is estimated as 1.5% of purchase price plus a fixed administration fee (commonly 825 SEK). This is a one-time purchase cost.

Monthly estimate includes loan interest, amortization (simple model by loan-to-value), yearly property fee/tax, utilities, home insurance, and a monthly share of one-time costs.
